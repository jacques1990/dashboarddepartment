Hard rules
  1. No written A+ setup means no simulated trade.
  2. A rule-breaking win is a bad decision; stop the session.
  3. After a win: journal it, take a 10-minute break, and do not increase size.
  4. After one loss: session over. Close the laptop, walk away, and do not trade again today.
  5. Never chase a loss, move the stop, or take “one last trade.”
  6. Three losing practice sessions: pause the strategy and review at least 20 logged examples.
  7. The goal is rule-following, not money or excitement.
Loss protection is automatic: one loss ends the day.
